Privacy Policy

Verdi does not collect, transmit or share any data.

  • The app has no network permission and never connects to the internet. There are no accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Everything you enter (grows, notes, photos, settings) is stored only on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your password. We never see your password and cannot recover it.
  • Photos are stripped of metadata (EXIF, including GPS location) before they are saved.
  • Reminders are scheduled locally on the device. No server is involved.
  • You can delete all data at any time in the app (Security → Destroy everything) or by uninstalling the app. Backups are created only when you export one yourself, and they stay encrypted.
  • The app is intended for adults (18+) and only for uses permitted by the law where you live.

If a future version adds optional online features, they will be off by default, require your explicit consent, and this policy will be updated before release.
